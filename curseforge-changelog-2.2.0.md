**109 new bounty items, and skill-locked items can now be handed to a villager.**

---

## Give a villager what it asked for, skill or no skill

In RLCraft, Reskillable locks items behind skill levels. A diamond shovel needs Gathering 16, so a villager asking for one couldn't be given it until you had the level. The lock blocked the sneak-click before the villager ever saw it.

Handing an item to a villager isn't *using* it, so bounty deliveries now go through regardless of skill locks. The locks still apply everywhere else: you still can't dig with that shovel until you've earned it.

## 109 new bounty items

The list grows from 152 to 261. Every new ID was checked against the RLCraft mod jars.

- **Low.** Leather, feathers, slimeballs, eggs, arrows, ink sacs, nether wart. Rustic olives, ironberries, tomatoes, chili peppers, ginseng and chamomile. Cooked venison, turkey and crab. Three more Lycanites meats. Purified water, ice chunks and magma chunks.

  - **Thirteen Lycanites meals.**

  - Others: parasite tendons and shells, warp and return scrolls, charcoal filters, crab and snail shells.
- **High.**
  - **Dragon scales in every colour and sea serpent scales in every colour.**
  - The Battle Burrito.
  - The Defiled Lands boss drops (Black Heart, Mourner and Destroyer essences, Remorseful Gem).
  - Shulker shells, dragon's breath, a totem of undying, a soulstone.

Items from mods you don't have are still skipped, so the list stays safe on any pack.

**Existing installs:** Forge keeps the bounty list already in your config. To get the new items, delete the `BOUNTY_ITEMS` block from `config/rlcraftvillagertomes.cfg` (or the whole file) and restart. Requests villagers have already rolled are unaffected either way.
