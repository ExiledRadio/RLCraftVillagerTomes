An asterisk marks a quantity overridden for that item rather than inherited from its band. Requests draw from the cheapest bands first and reach further up with each slot, so a first request is ordinary material and a fifth can ask for a dragon heart.

Slot progression — how a request escalates

A band is chosen by weight first, then an item evenly within it — so a band's odds are a decision rather than a side effect of how many items happen to be in it. Bands with nothing left to give are skipped and their weight redistributed. Slots past the last row keep using it.
