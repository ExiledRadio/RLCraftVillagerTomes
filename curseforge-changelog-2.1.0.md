**Villagers now sell you every slot up front.** You no longer have to fill one with a book before the next becomes available.

## Buy the whole villager before you risk anything

A librarian asks for its next bounty from the moment it is below the slot cap, whatever its existing slots are holding. Deliver, it opens a slot and immediately tells you what the one after that costs. Repeat four times and you have a five-slot villager that has never seen a book.

That is the point. A villager's odds come from how many slots it has opened — 30% on one slot, 70% on five, with a ceiling that climbs from 80% to 100% alongside it. Until now the only way to get there was to gamble books at the *worst* chances the mod offers, which is exactly backwards: the villager you most wanted to be safe was the one you had to be reckless with.

Now the preparation is the whole errand. Buy all five slots, bank a single glowing gem, and the first book you ever hand over is a certainty.

- Sneak-click empty-handed shows the outstanding bounty at any depth, not just on a full villager.
- Paying one slot off prints the next list in the same breath instead of making you click again.
- A quest log entry rolls onto the villager's new demand rather than being crossed off, so tracking one from its first slot to its fifth takes a single page.
- A villager at the cap says so once and never asks again.

Nothing else changed. Requests still never re-roll, bands still get richer with depth, and a villager wanting something you cannot get is still a villager you walk away from.

**Note on order:** a bounty is checked before a catalyst, so while a villager is still asking for glowing powder or ingots, those go towards the bounty rather than the odds. Once its slots are bought there is no demand left and every catalyst banks normally — which is the order you want anyway.

## Install

Delete `RLCraftVillagerTomes-2.0.0.jar` from your `mods` folder before adding this one. Two versions crash on startup with a duplicate mod id.

Worlds and villagers carry over untouched.
